Passage 1 · Weekend Library Hours

A neighborhood library recently changed its weekend schedule. In the past, it opened only on Saturday mornings, which made it difficult for many residents to visit. After receiving several requests, the library began opening on both Saturday and Sunday afternoons.

During the first two months, weekend attendance increased by almost 40 percent. Families with children became the largest new group of visitors, and the library also noticed that more people were borrowing books instead of only using the study area. Because of the positive response, the library plans to keep the new schedule for the rest of the year.

Passage 2 · A Change of Plan

Marina had planned to drive to a training session in another city, but her car would not start that morning. She called the training center to explain the problem and learned that the first session would also be available online. Instead of canceling, she joined the virtual session from home and arranged to have her car checked later that day.

The online option was not her original plan, but it allowed her to complete the first part of the training without losing a day. She decided to travel to the city the following morning after the mechanic confirmed that the car was safe to drive.
